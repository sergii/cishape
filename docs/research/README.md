# Research

This directory captures technologies, implementations, papers, product ideas, and architectural patterns that may be relevant to CIShape.

A research note is **not** an RFC, ADR, roadmap item, implementation plan, or product commitment.

The intended meaning is:

> We found something in the world that may matter. We want to preserve the idea and understand it before deciding whether CIShape should use, support, integrate with, or ignore it.

Research notes may contain:

- prior art;
- competing approaches;
- interesting implementation techniques;
- vocabulary worth reusing;
- questions raised by an external system;
- possible experiments;
- links for later investigation.

A note should clearly separate:

- **Observed** - what the referenced system actually does;
- **Possible relevance** - why CIShape might care;
- **Unknowns** - what we have not validated;
- **Decision status** - normally "none".

Promote a research note into an RFC or ADR only when there is a concrete CIShape decision to make.
